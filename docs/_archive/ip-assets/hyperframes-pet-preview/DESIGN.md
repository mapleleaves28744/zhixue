## Style Prompt

Dark education-tech desktop-pet showcase for Zhixue Workshop. The visual language uses cute sticker mascots, deep blue-violet canvas, soft card panels, subtle constellation route lines, warm source glows, and restrained motion. The mascots are the focus; UI chrome stays quiet and functional.

## Colors

- Background night: `#111427`
- Panel ink: `#1B2140`
- Wiki teal: `#7DDDD2`
- Compass amber: `#F5C96B`
- Quiz lavender: `#B7A4FF`
- Soft text: `#F6F4FF`
- Muted text: `#AEB6D8`

## Typography

- Primary: `Inter`, `Segoe UI`, `Arial`, sans-serif
- Use compact labels and short status copy only.

## Motion

- Idle: gentle breathing, tiny vertical bob, low-amplitude glow.
- Remind: quick bell-like pop and warm pulse.
- Focus: steady slow scale, reduced movement, source-card sweep.
- Done: bright star burst, confident bounce, checkmark accent.

## What NOT to Do

- Do not use harsh neon, full black, or rainbow gradients.
- Do not cover the mascot with large UI labels.
- Do not use photorealistic lighting or 3D rendering.
- Do not introduce unrelated characters or change each mascot's core identity.
